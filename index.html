Welcome to Majestic Park Inn

A quieter way to experience modern hospitality.

Rooted in residential warmth, sculpted natural timber, and clean architectural daylight, Majestic Park Inn was imagined as a tranquil sanctuary. Here, the hurried tempo of the city recedes behind fluted linen curtains, hand-loomed wool rugs, and the quiet scent of seasoned walnut.

Every corner celebrates contemporary Indian craftsmanship: cane joinery crafted by master carpenters, bespoke textured ceramics, and custom lighting designed to cast a soft, restorative evening glow.
