## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, tactile digital experience for an upscale boutique hotel. Rooted in residential comfort, architectural dining, and contemporary Indian artisanal craftsmanship, the aesthetic balances the quiet authority of an architectural monograph with the warmth of an intimate private residence.

The visual direction operates at the intersection of **Editorial Luxury** and **Warm Tactility**:
- Generous, deliberate negative space mirroring sun-drenched courtyards and terracotta breezeways.
- Thoughtful balance of organic warmth (linen, raw plaster, deep walnut) with crisp, tailored typographic precision.
- Asymmetry inspired by traditional Indian architectural jalis, carved colonnades, and modern spatial framing.
- Avoidance of generic corporate hospitality tropes (such as harsh bright blues, sterile cold whites, and heavy gradient drop shadows) in favor of layered natural tones, razor-thin hairlines, and sensory imagery.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from raw earth, aged teak, beaten brass, and bleached linen. It relies primarily on a tonal dialogue between deep wood tones and sunlit stone, employing warm terracotta orange strictly as an intentional focal point.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Page Canvas (`#F5F1E8` Warm Ivory):** The foundational substrate across all top-level viewports, offering an unbleached, paper-like warmth that prevents screen glare.
- **Secondary Surfaces:** 
  - `#FAF9F5` (Linen White): Elevated card planes, active form fields, and modal containers.
  - `#C7B49A` (Warm Taupe): Section transitions, tinted callouts, and muted structural backgrounds.
- **Primary Structure & High-Contrast Typography (`#553B2E` Deep Walnut):** The primary color anchor for display typography, bespoke iconography, and grounding structural containers (e.g., dining spotlights and footers).
- **Body Copy & Utilities (`#292827` Charcoal):** Applied to running text, legal lines, and system notifications for sustained legibility against ivory and linen.
- **Primary Accent (`#E87532` Burnt Orange):** Applied strictly to conversion touchpoints, booking triggers, and key interactive highlights (under 10% total screen real estate).
- **Subtle Metallic Accent (`#B3945A` Muted Gold):** Reserved for star tiers, bespoke architectural badges, suite numbers, and deliberate decorative hairline framing.
- **Hairline Dividers (`rgba(85, 59, 46, 0.15)` Walnut Tint):** Ultra-delicate physical separation lines that echo tailored millwork and fine joinery.

## Typography

Typography acts as the central architectural structure of the layout. The pairing pairs the classical grace of **EB Garamond** with the rhythmic geometric legibility of **Manrope**.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines & Display:** Handled exclusively with EB Garamond in regular and medium weights. Headlines are rendered with tight negative tracking to maintain the density and presence of hand-set metal type. Italic variants may be introduced sparingly for single evocative adjectives or Indian cultural vernacular terms (e.g., *Baithak*, *Thikri*).
- **Body & Editorial Narratives:** Manrope brings a functional, contemporary counterweight. Its generous x-height and open apertures prevent visual fatigue across room descriptions, historical narratives, and dining menus.
- **Labels, Navigation, and Microcopy:** Styled in all-caps Manrope with wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`). This creates a crisp metadata layer that contrasts with the editorial serif titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an architectural portfolio: rhythm is slow, deliberate, and paced with unhurried transitions.

### Grid System & Form Factors
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 12-column asymmetric grid with max container width of `1440px`. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem`, while outer margins expand gracefully (`4rem` minimum). Key editorial stories intentionally span uneven column distributions (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns for narrative copy).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standard 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Asymmetric pairings collapse into stacked rhythm, preserving 4:3 and 16:9 editorial aspect ratios.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Grids convert to vertical chronological streams with horizontal swipe affordances for visual lookbooks and suite inventories.

### Spacing Principles
- Never crowd photography against typographical headings. Editorial content requires at least `space-lg` to breathe.
- Full-bleed sections (such as moody walnut dining blocks) deliberately bypass the standard container margins, spanning edge-to-edge with internal padding aligned with the primary grid guides.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed through **architectural stratification, tactile planes, and ultra-low-contrast hairlines**, completely avoiding heavy synthetic blur drops or generic drop-shadow tiers.

### Structural Depth Techniques
- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#F5F1E8`. Floating elements (such as room cards or booking fields) sit atop this ground using `#FAF9F5` (Linen White), creating a quiet, non-jarring contrast reminiscent of paper resting on raw lime plaster.
- **Architectural Dark Insets:** Key experience chapters (such as sensory dining and the evening cocktail salon) invert the spatial balance into a dark container (`#553B2E` Deep Walnut), evoking ambient candlelight and polished wood.
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is punctuated through crisp `1px` walnut hairline borders (`rgba(85, 59, 46, 0.15)`). Where elevated surfaces overlap imagery, an inner `1px` outline tinted with `#B3945A` (Muted Gold at 30% opacity) signals bespoke luxury.
- **Ambient Shadows:** When interactive modals or floating bars decouple from the page scroll, they utilize an ultra-diffused, walnut-tinted ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(85, 59, 46, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language as its default across cards, buttons, images, and dialogs.

### Geometry & Form Language
- Architectural precision requires straight lines, sharp junctions, and right angles (`border-radius: 0px`). This honors the stone joinery, jali screen profiles, and geometric courtyard plans of Indian modernist architecture.
- Cards, inputs, most buttons, interactive sheets, and picture frames maintain crisp 90-degree corners.
- Exceptions are applied strictly to circular functional counters (e.g., guest counters or directional carousel arrows), which remain pure geometric circles (`50%` radius) rather than pill capsules.

### Component Exception — Architectural Booking Bar
The **Booking Bar** (`#booking-engine`) is the single deliberate curve exception, following the **Soft Architectural Curve** (Option 1) treatment:
- **Outer container shell:** `border-radius: 20px` — echoes the curved arches, bouclé barrel armchairs, and amber glass globes of the hotel architecture.
- **Inner form grid:** `border-radius: 16px` with `overflow: hidden` so field backgrounds clip cleanly inside the shell without sharp overflows.
- **Search / CTA button:** `border-radius` applied only to the right edge (`border-top-right-radius: 16px; border-bottom-right-radius: 16px`) — the button blends into the outer shell curvature gracefully while its left edge remains flush with the field dividers.
- **Psychological rationale:** Softens the interface to evoke warmth without compromising the overall architectural discipline of the page.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#E87532` (Burnt Orange), text `#FAF9F5` (Linen White), zero radius, uppercase tracking (`button-text`). Hover states darken gracefully to `#D16222` with a `200ms` ease.
- **Secondary / Architectural Button:** Crisp border `1px solid #553B2E`, transparent background, text `#553B2E`. On hover, fills with `#553B2E` and inverts text to `#FAF9F5`.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Inline text link styled in Charcoal or Deep Walnut with a persistent `1px` underline offset by `4px`, accented by an arrow glyph (`→`) that translates `4px` on hover.

### Floating Navigation Bar
- Positioned pinned to top, decoupled with `space-sm` margins on desktop.
- Resting state over imagery: translucent background with light text.
- Scroll state: transitions cleanly via backdrop blur (`blur(12px)`) over `#F5F1E8` ivory surface at 90% opacity, bordered underneath by the walnut hairline `rgba(85, 59, 46, 0.15)`.
- Features an ultra-refined logo mark, uppercase navigation links (`label-caps`), and a compact direct-booking action.

### Architectural Booking Bar
- Modular, horizontal desktop component sitting flush or slightly overlapping the hero canvas.
- Compartmentalized with vertical `1px` hairline dividers separating: Check-in, Check-out, Guests, and Promo Code.
- Labels sit above values in `label-caps` in `#553B2E` (60% opacity); input selection displays in `#292827` using Manrope regular.
- Concludes with a full-height Burnt Orange `#E87532` "Check Availability" trigger button.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- Flat fields on `#FAF9F5` with `1px` bottom or full-perimeter hairline borders in walnut tint.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#B3945A` (Muted Gold) without default browser outlines.
- Datepicker / Availability Calendar: Clean grid showing calendar days; selected date range indicated by a continuous `#FAF9F5` block on a muted taupe background with endpoints highlighted in `#E87532`.

### Asymmetric Room & Suite Showcase Cards
- Images framed in strict ratios (3:4 portrait or 16:10 cinematic) with no border-radius.
- Cards present typography staggered below or partially overlapping the image corner on a `#FAF9F5` linen tag.
- Includes micro-amenities listed in `label-caps` with subtle gold bullet dividers (`•`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls: crisp square nested selector (`14px x 14px`) with a solid inner `#553B2E` square when active, honoring the geometric theme.
- Checkboxes: `14px x 14px` square with clean hairline border, checking with a minimalist `#E87532` fill and sharp white check icon.

### Moody Walnut Dining & Tasting Sections
- Enclosed full-width container with `#553B2E` background, carrying creamy `#FAF9F5` headlines and `#C7B49A` body narrative.
- Accompanied by subtle geometric line motifs evocative of contemporary Indian fretwork, drawn with `1px` gold lines (`#B3945A` at 20% opacity).

### Lightbox & Imagery Viewer
- Fullscreen overlay rendered in `#292827` at 95% opacity.
- Minimal hairline utility controls (Close, Slide indices `01 / 12` in `label-caps`).
- Captions set in EB Garamond regular centered below high-resolution photography.
